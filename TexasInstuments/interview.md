Texas Instruments Interview Experience | Round 1

The interview lasted around 1 hour and covered DSA and SQL.

1. DSA – Wildcard Matching

I was asked a wildcard matching problem. I initially explained the brute-force approach, but the interviewer expected a recursive DP approach. I explained the recursive approach and completed the iterative DP implementation.

2. SQL – Indexing and Query Optimization

The second question focused on SQL indexing. I was given a query involving a country ID and asked to explain why it was running slowly and how I would optimize it.

The interviewer then asked what could be done beyond regular indexing. We discussed composite indexing and how it could help improve query performance.

Overall Experience: The interview focused on problem-solving, the ability to explain approaches, dynamic programming, and SQL query optimization. It was a 1-hour technical discussion
