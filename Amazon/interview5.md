Amazon SDE 1 Interview Experience

Applied through a referral. Gave the Online Assessment in July and received the interview call in September. The interviews were conducted offline at the Amazon Bangalore office.

Round 1)
Was asked two DSA questions:

- Binary Search — variation of Koko Eating Bananas
- Graph — Shortest Path problem

The round also included questions based on Amazon Leadership Principles (LPs).

Round 2)
Was asked:

- A variation of the Celebrity Problem
- LRU Cache
- Questions about my projects

Round 3)
Was asked some Generative AI-related questions, followed by Design a Ticket Booking System.

Initially, I approached it as an HLD problem, but the interviewer expected more of an LLD/code-level design discussion. This was the area where I struggled.

Interview Feedback:
Strong in DSA, but weak in LLD/System Design.

Verdict — Rejection
