
1. Online Assessment (OA): There are usually 4–5 sections, and you need to complete them within the given time limit.
2. Case Study Round: If you clear the OA, you may receive an interview call for the case study round.
3. Case Study Preparation: You will need to solve the case study questions on your own. Make sure you understand and remember the questions you encountered during the test.
4. Case Study Interview: If you are shortlisted after the case study round, you will have an interview based on the case study. The interviewer may ask questions related to the case studies from the test.
5. Technical Interview: If you clear the case study interview, you will be called for the technical interview round.
6. HR Round: The final round is usually an HR round, although this round may or may not be conducted depending on the hiring process.
