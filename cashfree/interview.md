Hi Everyone, sharing my interview experience with the Cashfree for the SDE-1 Backend Role.I was contacted by the HR directly.

R1: DSA - 1 Hour
This round was based on the Hackerrank, where one needs to solve 2 DSA Questions (Non leetcode) and passing all the TCs. Some follow-ups were also asked. Round was eliminatory.

R2: LLD
Was asked to design Google Calendar for this. Interviewer expected code and classes structure first. After that we talked about how to handle concurrency, and the interview ended with some DBMS questions. This was on hackerrank as well.

R3: Hiring Manager Round. 
This was conducted offline at Gurgaon Office. It was based on my experience, kind of work I had done at the previous company, why I wanted to switch, etc. Questions such as internal working of kafka, Internal working of B-Trees were asked, along with some behavioural questions.

Final Verdict: I was rejected. Learned a lot as this was my first offline intvw and HM round as well.
