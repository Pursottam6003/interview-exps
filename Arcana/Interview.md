Round 1: Online Assessment (HackerRank)
- 2 coding questions.
- First question: Easy to Medium.
- Second question: Medium to Hard.
- Time allotted: 1 hour.
- I was able to solve both questions within the given time and subsequently received an interview invitation.

Round 2: Technical Interview (1 Hour)
This round covered:

- DSA and problem-solving.
- Software Testing concepts and questions.
- Resume and previous experience discussion.
- CS fundamentals.

Round 3: DSA Round (1 Hour)

- 2 coding questions on HackerRank.
- Both questions were of Medium difficulty.

Round 4: CTO Round (Final Round)
The final round was with the CTO and primarily focused on:

- Core CS fundamentals.
- Discussion about previous work experience.
- Technical discussions around projects and background.

A few preparation tips:
- Practise DSA regularly, especially Medium-level problems.
- Be thorough with your CS fundamentals.
- Understand software testing concepts.
- Be confident about everything mentioned in your resume and previous experience.
